## What this establishes

- The whole no-phone journey runs on commit 7c4a3bc with the pool attached to the same registry the app and the bridge use: one attest opens both doors, one revoke closes both, and both refusals are decoded on chain and on the page.
- Subscribe after revoke reverts with NotEligible() (selector 0xf8eb54de); the portal does not send it (button disabled), the CLI cannot send it (gas estimation refuses).
- Swap after revoke reverts inside PermissionedHooks.beforeSwap with Unauthorized() wrapped by the PoolManager (WrappedError, HookCallFailed), three times: the CLI replay, the portal click, the Playwright spec. All three mined refusals cost 77809 gas.
- The swap amounts are what the pool state gives, not a fixed number: 75.569 NDF and 63.961 NDF for 100 mUSD after the probe's 90.653 NDF, each matching the page's slot0 estimate to four decimals.
- The four gates pass on the same clean tree.
