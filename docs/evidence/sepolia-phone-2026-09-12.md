# Official wallet journey on Sepolia, 2026-09-12 (sanitized)

Every transaction below was mined on Sepolia (chain id 11155111) against the contracts in docs/deployments/sepolia-2026-09-10.md, through the stack started by `scripts/browser-real-wallet-up.sh --deployment`. Run directory (private, gitignored): docs/evidence/private/runs/20260912-110431-browser. This file holds the app commit, addresses, transaction hashes with Etherscan links, gas, timings from the logs, the chain and the Present card, and decoded reverts. No keys, no presentation, no pickup token. Figures marked "from the card" come from the investor portal's page text pasted by the builder after the run (page header Sepolia, block 11688156).

## What this establishes

The readiness review of 2026-09-11 (wiki page review-readiness-2026-09-11.md, section "One acceptance run", private wiki, not in this repository) asked for six steps on one address, registry, chain and browser tab. Against this run:

1. Record the wallet version, the commit, the deployment addresses and the chain; start with no evidence and no approval. Met except the wallet version string: commit 5f17c3a, chain 11155111 and the addresses are above; block 11687856 shows no decision and no approval on the fresh investor. The official wallet's version string was not recorded (builder to add, as in the 2026-09-08 record).
2. Sign the session, present the sample credential from the official wallet, generate the proof in the browser, obtain a successful `attestWithProof` receipt. Met: the session was signed by the page's dev signer, the verifier stored the official wallet's encrypted response (09:09:49), the proof was made in the tab, and tx 0x4ea1b1a4… is a status 1 `attestWithProof` with the investor as subject and the registry's verifier pinned to the sandbox issuer. Boundary: the session signature and the submission used the dev signer and the bridge key from the builder's `.env`, not a wallet extension.
3. Before approval, `statusOf = (true, false, false, expiry)` and `isEligible = false`, both doors closed, read-only refusal checks captured. Met for the state: block 11687955 reads exactly that. The read-only refusal of the Subscribe door is captured as an `eth_call` against block 11687955's state made after the run (NotEligible, 0xf8eb54de), not during it; the Swap door's read-only refusal before approval was not captured (the page showed it closed; no call was recorded).
4. Approve separately, confirm eligibility, the free subscription and a purchase with test mUSD with a receipt and token transfer. Met: tx 0x45665996… (Approved event), `isEligible` true at 11688109, tx 0x00c20b41… mints 100 NDF for no ETH, tx 0xda990178… swaps 100 mUSD for 75.569267032175299294 NDF with the FundToken `Transfer` to the investor in the receipt.
5. Revoke manually, confirm the disabled Subscribe control, its decoded read-only refusal and the pool's actual refusal; distinguish mined rejection from never sent. Met with one caveat: tx 0xd62cdf8d… (Revoked event); the Subscribe button was greyed on the builder's screen (no screenshot in the run directory) and no subscribe was sent (nonce sequence); the read-only refusal is the `eth_call` at block 11688138 (NotEligible) made after the run; the pool's refusal is tx 0x85e554b8…, mined with status 0 and decoded to Unauthorized() inside beforeSwap.
6. Save an evidence record with exact hashes, decoded results and measured durations. This file. Durations: from the card (worker witness 1.25 s, bb_init 1.8 s, prove 11.0 s, verify 4.0 s, pickup to attested 40 s, request creation to attested 193.5 s including about 153 s of waiting for the scan), from the logs and the chain (verifier relay line to attestation block 35 s, swap sequence 48 s, refused sequence 36 s, block times for everything else).
