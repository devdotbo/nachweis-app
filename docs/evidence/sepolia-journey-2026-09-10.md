# No-phone journey on Sepolia through the app stack, 2026-09-10 (sanitized)

The complete investor journey on the Sepolia deployment of 2026-09-10, driven with the deployment's own keys instead of a phone: attest by operator, approve, subscribe, swap through the permissioned Uniswap v4 pool, revoke, subscribe refused, swap refused, re-approve. Every transaction below was mined on Sepolia (chain id 11155111) against the contracts in docs/deployments/sepolia-2026-09-10.md, through the stack started by `scripts/browser-real-wallet-up.sh --deployment`. Run directory (private, gitignored): docs/evidence/private/runs/20260910-210601-browser. This file holds the app commit, addresses, transaction hashes with Etherscan links, gas, timings and decoded reverts. No keys, no tunnel hostname, no home paths.

Purpose: browser-pool-journey-2026-09-10.md proved the same journey on a throwaway fork. This run records the journey on Sepolia itself, on the main branch at the commit that carries the deployment record, and leaves the investor in the approved state for the phone rehearsal.

## What this establishes

- The whole no-phone journey runs on Sepolia on commit 57a4be5 against the recorded deployment, through the same stack script, bridge and app the phone rehearsal uses: one attest opens both doors, one revoke closes both, and both refusals are decoded on chain and on the page.
- Subscribe after revoke reverts with NotEligible() (selector 0xf8eb54de); the portal does not send it (button disabled), the CLI cannot send it (gas estimation refuses).
- Swap after revoke reverts inside PermissionedHooks.beforeSwap with Unauthorized() wrapped by the PoolManager (WrappedError, HookCallFailed), twice on Sepolia: the spec's click and the portal click. Both mined refusals cost 77809 gas, the same as the three on the fork.
- The swap received 90.652862473832711386 NDF for 100 mUSD on the untouched pool, matching the page's slot0 estimate to four decimals and the fork probe's first-swap amount (90.652862473832711386 NDF on the fork's identical pool state).
- `e2e/swap.spec.ts` runs unchanged against a Sepolia stack through env.json once the investor is eligible; it passes with the 90 s per-step timeouts at about 12 s per block.
- Investor spend for the whole journey was 0.00103 ETH; 0.04897 ETH remain for the phone leg.

## Deviations from the runbook

- The relay verifier was a local stub through G0_ENV, not the real verifier plus cloudflared tunnel; the runbook's "Sepolia run" describes the real one. Nothing in this run used the verifier beyond the stack's health and relay probe.
- Bridge health reports `proof_mode: "mock"`; the runbook's expected bring-up line shows `proof_mode: null`. No proof was submitted in this run.
- The spec's swap execute cost 228030 gas on Sepolia against 210798 on the fork (cold storage on the real chain); the refused execute cost the same 77809.
- The investor keeps 100 mUSD from the spec's second faucet mint; the runbook's click sequence mints only when the balance is 0, so the phone rehearsal's Swap click will skip the mint.
