### 8. Describe how AI tools were used in your project

The team is one human builder. The code in the repository (contracts, bridge service, front end, SP1 guest and host, Noir circuit adaptation, companion and mobile provers, scripts) and the documentation were written by Claude Code agents, Anthropic models Fable 5.1, run as a lead session with teammates from written work packages; the agents also ran the tests and emulator runs and recorded the numbers. Independent reviews at decision points came from OpenAI GPT model Astra, xAI Grok and Z.ai GLM models; they changed wording and the order of work, not code. The builder set the product and the privacy boundary, made and dated every decision, holds the keys and domains, ran every official wallet run on the phone, records the video in the builder's own voice and submits the forms. Full record with specs, prompts and planning artifacts: docs/ai-attribution.md in the repository.
