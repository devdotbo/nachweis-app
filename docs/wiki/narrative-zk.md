# EUDI ZK narrative check

Purpose: the builder wants to say, in the pitch and in the submission text, roughly: "EUDI plans zero-knowledge presentations, but the EU has not fully specified the ZK parts yet, so we make the proof on the user's device now, until the specification lands; then we will see whether it needs its own EUDI ZK wallet, whether the official wallet can do it, or whether we stay in this lane." This page checks every factual claim in that sentence against primary sources and proposes wordings that pass the honesty rules in /Users/bioharz/git/ethglobal/nachweis/wiki/product.md. Fetches were done by subagents on 2026-09-08; the quotes below are verbatim from the fetched pages.

## Summary (OPINION)

The narrative is true in substance and wrong in three details. True: the regulation asks member states to integrate zero-knowledge proof (a recital, not an article), the ARF says no ZKP has been selected and support is expected after launch, no source says the official German test wallet can prove today, and our proof is made on the user's machine. Wrong or risky: "the EU has not specified" understates what exists (Topic 53 with nine requirements, three technical specifications, a Longfellow ZK type name, a 2026 refinement round); "the user's device" is the user's Mac, not the phone; and "own EUDI ZK wallet" is an option the builder already rejected on 2026-09-07 for certification reasons. The wordings in the last section keep the substance, drop the three details, and say where the proof is made.

## Certification: why "own EUDI ZK wallet" must not be promised

The option "then we will see whether it needs its own EUDI ZK wallet" was rejected by the builder on 2026-09-07 (FACT, /Users/bioharz/git/ethglobal/nachweis/wiki/decisions.md: "own wallet as product (cannot hold a sandbox PID; certification not before 2028 in Germany)"). The sources behind that decision were re-fetched on 2026-09-08 and hold:

- FACT, Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 Art 5a(2) (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32024R1183, fetched 2026-09-08): wallets are provided "directly by a Member State", "under a mandate from a Member State", or "independently of a Member State but recognised by that Member State". Art 5c(1): conformity of wallets "shall be certified by conformity assessment bodies designated by Member States."
- FACT, Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2981 Art 4(4)(a) (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2024/2981/oj, fetched 2026-09-08): "only providers referred to in Article 5a(2) of Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 are eligible to be issued certificates under the national certification schemes". Recital 5: "wallets are to be certified against assurance level high".
- FACT, BMDS (https://bmds.bund.de/themen/digitaler-staat/digitale-identitaeten/eudi-wallet, fetched 2026-09-08): "Alternative Wallet-Anbieter werden zu einem späteren Zeitpunkt die Möglichkeit erhalten, ihre Lösungen als EUDI-Wallets in Deutschland zertifizieren und offiziell anerkennen zu lassen." and "Nach derzeitiger Planung soll diese Option rund zwölf Monate nach Einführung der staatlichen EUDI-Wallet bereitstehen." With the state wallet due "in early 2027" (https://eudi-wallet.gov.de/en/faq, fetched 2026-09-08; 2027-01-02 per Drucksache 21/7319, fetched 2026-09-06), "around early 2028" is our arithmetic, not a published date.
- FACT, Drucksache 21/7408 of 2026-07-29, government bill Digitale-Identitäten-Gesetz (https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/074/2107408.pdf, fetched 2026-09-08, marked Vorabfassung): EBDIG section 6(1): recognition of an independent wallet "erfolgt auf Grund einer ergangenen Verordnung nach § 22 Absatz 2 Nummer 3 die Anerkennung auf Antrag", so the procedure exists only after a BMDS ordinance with Bundesrat consent (section 22(1)). Legislative status on https://bmds.bund.de/service/gesetzgebungsverfahren/digitale-identitaetengesetz (fetched 2026-09-08): Bundestag readings and conclusion "Noch nicht erfolgt" (the page is behind the Drucksache, which already carries the Bundesrat opinion). The bill contains no occurrence of "zero-knowledge", "Nullwissen" or "ZKP".
- FACT, https://eudi-wallet.gov.de/en/ecosystem (fetched 2026-09-08): "The EUDI Wallet ecosystem enables alternative wallet providers in addition to the state EUDI Wallet." with the prerequisite "that all providers meet the same technical standards and legal requirements."
- FACT, the German sandbox has no wallet-provider role and its PID issuer is locked to the official wallet by wallet unit attestation (/Users/bioharz/git/ethglobal/new-path/eudi-zk/raw/teammate-memos/2026-09-06-wallet-ecosystem-openness.md, summary items 3 and 7, sources fetched 2026-09-06). An own wallet could never hold a sandbox PID.

Consequence (OPINION): an "own EUDI ZK wallet" is a certified wallet at assurance level high, recognised by Germany under an ordinance that does not exist yet, and it cannot be tested with a state PID today. It is not a hackathon option and not a credible "next step" in front of a judge who knows eIDAS. The future options that can be stated truthfully are: (a) the official wallet ships a ZK scheme (ARF: expected after launch, no scheme selected), and the registry accepts that proof through its pluggable verifier interface; (b) until then, the proof is made on the investor's side over the wallet's ordinary presentation, on the laptop today, in the browser or on the phone when those provers are verified. Do not list "our own wallet" as an option. If asked, answer: "certification is restricted to providers a member state recognises; in Germany that procedure follows an ordinance planned about a year after the state wallet launches; we build on the relying-party side, which is open today."

Claims seen only in search-result summaries and not verified by fetch on 2026-09-08 (do not use): a von der Leyen statement of 2026-04-15 on ZK in the age-verification app; a SPRIND and HPI pilot from November 2026 to June 2027; a Bundestag statement that ZKPs are not available at the launch of the state wallet.

## Risks of the builder's phrasing (what a knowledgeable judge could object to)

1. "The EU has not specified ZK" is falsifiable in one click. A judge who knows the ARF opens Annex 2 Topic 53 (nine requirements), TS13 (a Longfellow type name) or the Age Verification app changelog (Longfellow shipped 2026-09-03, experimental) and concludes we did not read the framework. Say instead what the ARF itself says: no scheme selected, support expected after launch, refinement round in 2026.
2. "On the user's device" is heard as "on the phone". Our recorded proof runs on the user's Mac in a companion CLI; the phone provers ran on an emulator and a simulator; the browser proof is a spike with nothing committed tonight. The honesty rule in product.md ("where the proof is made must be stated on screen") forces "on the user's computer" or "on the user's laptop" plus, if WP26 turns green, "in the browser".
3. "Own EUDI ZK wallet" contradicts the recorded decision of 2026-09-07 and the certification facts below. A judge who knows eIDAS asks who would certify it; a judge who knows the German sandbox asks how it would get a PID. Keep the wallet out of the list of future options, or name it only as "a wallet that ships a ZK scheme", without "our".
4. "Zero-knowledge presentation" from EUDI is a wallet-side concept (the wallet proves to the relying party). Ours is relying-party-side: the wallet still discloses three claims (SD-JWT); the companion on the user's side then proves over that disclosure so that the verifier and the chain never see the claims. A judge may say "that is not what the ARF means by ZKP". Answer it before it is asked: "the ARF's ZKP is wallet-side and unselected; ours proves over today's ordinary presentation, on the user's side, so the relying party sees nothing. When the wallet proves, the registry takes that proof instead."
5. "Unlinkability" as a regulation quote: the published text says "unlikeability" (Art 5a(16)(b)). Paraphrase, do not quote.
6. "Nothing about you on chain" is banned by product.md; predicate bits and expiry bound to an address are personal data. Say "no identity documents on chain, and nothing we could use to find her".
7. The SPRIND sentence. "SPRIND is building ZK" is unverified as a programme; what is verifiable is a SoK paper by a SPRIND author (eprint 2026/330). Do not say "the thing SPRIND is lacking" (banned in product.md) and do not attribute a plan to SPRIND.
8. "Until the specification lands" sounds like a complaint about the EU and dates the product. Frame the gap as the reason the product exists now: issuers need eligibility proofs before wallet-side ZKP ships, and the chain side does not change when the prover moves.

## Wordings that pass the honesty rules

All three are true as of 2026-09-08 with the proof on the user's Mac. Bracketed alternatives apply only if WP26 turns green (browser proof verified against the committed VK) before the video is cut; otherwise delete the bracket.

Video, 40 words (spoken over beat 3 or beat 7):

"EU law asks wallets to add zero-knowledge proofs; the framework has not picked a scheme yet. Today the proof is made on the investor's computer, over the official test wallet's presentation. When wallets prove, the registry accepts that proof instead."

(40 words.)

Submission, "how it's made", 120 words:

"eIDAS 2 tells member states to integrate zero-knowledge proofs into the EUDI wallet, and the Architecture and Reference Framework lists what a ZK scheme must satisfy, but as of v3.0.0 no scheme is selected and wallet-side support is expected after launch. Attestat does not wait. The official German test wallet presents an ordinary selective-disclosure SD-JWT; our verifier relays the encrypted response unopened; a companion on the investor's laptop decrypts it and proves in a Noir circuit that a PID signed by the pinned sandbox issuer key, bound to this crypto address, says over 18 (4.4 s on an M3 Max). Only that proof reaches the chain. Proof verifiers are pluggable, so a wallet-side ZK proof plugs in when one ships. [Browser proving is a running spike.]"

(120 words without the bracket, counting "4.4 s" as one item; 121 if the unit is counted separately.)

README, one sentence:

"Today the zero-knowledge proof over the official test wallet's presentation is made on the investor's own computer by the Attestat companion, with the verifier relaying the encrypted response unopened; the registry's proof interface is pluggable, so a wallet-side proof replaces it once EUDI wallets ship a ZK scheme."
