## Question 2: fastest way to a million

### What all three jurors said independently

- None of the 22 is a millionaire machine. The jury ranked a video; the income memos scored the products.
- The hackathon is a door, not money. Realistic prize stack 1,000 to 5,000 USD. 0 of 100 sampled sponsor winners appear in that sponsor's grantee, hire or pilot lists; 0 of 45 finalists raised (COUNT, sponsor-winners-aftermath memo).
- Fastest expected cash: subcontract the certified sandbox verifier into ISO 27001 EUDI vendors (Lissi, walt.id, Sphereon, Animo, A-Trust, youniqx, CRYPTAS, Procivis). Day rate 800 to 1,200 EUR (CLAIM, unverified for Rust plus identity). Procivis and walt.id roles as the employment fallback. A living, not a million.
- The pick, if one of the 22 must be picked because existence builds on it: issuer-side EUDI acceptance. Reasons: it is the only direction that feeds non-dilutive funding (deep-tech story), it builds on assets that cannot be copied in a weekend (registrar certificate to 2027-06-02, sandbox access, Longfellow ZK verifier, EUDI-ON reputation), and a buyer class for the company exists in 2027 when private relying parties must accept the wallet (Art 5f, 2027-12-24; member-state wallets 2026-12-24; CLAIM per income-paths section 1.6).
- Not the pick: Crossfoot as a front door (better finding, unpitchable, long enterprise sale), Work Order as a company (a feature of Request or Deel), a 50-cent boolean sold into DeFi (Coinbase and Keyring price it at zero), a token.

### Where gpt corrected both (accepted)

- Certainty. "None of the 22 can make you a millionaire" is stronger than the evidence. The screens measured novelty, sponsor fit and demo quality, not commercial ceiling. Correct sentence: none of the 22 shows a path to a million in the evidence we have.
- One corpus, three readers. Agreement came from the same memos. That is consistency, not customer validation. Every price is a comparable, not a quote.
- The aftermath count measured sponsor-channel conversion. It does not say hackathon starts do not become companies. 1inch (ETHGlobal New York 2019) and Pinata (ETHBerlin 2018, later an 18 million USD Series A) are counterexamples (CLAIM, gpt's links: https://1inch.com/blog/post/five-years-of-groundbreaking-web3-innovation and https://www.huschblackwell.com/inthenews/husch-blackwell-guides-pinata-in-series-a-offering, cited 2026-09-07, not refetched by claude). Both fit the shared framing: the hackathon was the door, the product was the money. Survivors, so they say nothing about probability.
- Grants, revenue, valuation and personal wealth are four ledgers. Grok's answer and claude's slid between them.
- ETHOnline's rubric (technicality, originality, practicality, usability, wow) does not measure willingness to pay. The business question adds something the competition does not measure; it does not replace the build.

### Where claude held against gpt

- The question was fastest, not possible. Copyable software with paying incumbents is a slower path, not a closed one. gpt did not dispute the ranking, only the wording of the exclusion.
- gpt's three steps are the existing plan plus one addition. Step 1 is the Sep 8 gate. Step 3 is the pilot letter-of-intent kill criterion already in the verdict page, dated 2027-03-31. Step 2 is new: see the action below.
