# Synthesis

Written by the lead from the four memos. Every number below is traceable to a memo; the memos hold the URLs.

## The verdict in six lines

1. The direction is buildable this week in an honest form. The zero-knowledge part is buildable only as a labelled second tier, not on the phone.
2. The pitch must not be "compliance layer" or "KYC-gated pool". COUNT: of 302 identity, KYC, compliance and personhood projects across 5,446 showcase entries, 106 won sponsor prizes and 6 of 131 finalists came from the group; every finalist used identity as a mechanic inside a product a non-expert wanted, and no compliance-layer framing ever reached a final.
3. The lead scene therefore has to be something a crypto-native person wants to do and is currently locked out of or has to dox themselves for. The strongest candidate with demand evidence is a token sale or airdrop that must exclude jurisdictions (the Aztec sale verified about 17,000 people with zkPassport). Pool admission stays as the reuse beat and the Uniswap sponsor, not the lead.
4. The legal claim that survives: a predicate is sufficient wherever the duty is the predicate (age, residency, jurisdiction, address control, sanctions signal for a non-obliged issuer). It is never sufficient for full customer due diligence, which needs the identification data set and five-year retention (AMLR Art 22 and Art 77). Say "eligibility, not onboarding".
5. Austria adds nothing usable this week except the A-SIT Plus demo PID as a second-country flavour and the Austrian passport for a passport-chip comparison clip. ID Austria is login only behind an accreditation gate. Do not spend a day on it.
6. The moderator's "why not zkPassport" answer is not true today and becomes true in 2027. The honest stage version: "zkPassport needs a chipped passport and a separate app; by 2027 every EU citizen has a state wallet by law; we built for that day, on the sandbox wallet that exists today."

## The product, stated once

One attestation, many doors. A person presents their state ID wallet once. A verifier that runs inside a Chainlink Confidential Workflow enclave reduces it to booleans (over 18, EU resident, not sanctioned, address controlled) and writes them, with an expiry and a revocation handle, against the wallet address. Any contract can read them. In the video: door one, a token sale that must exclude non-EU and sanctioned buyers admits her; door two, a Uniswap permissioned pool admits the same address without a second check; door three, the verifier revokes and both doors close. Nothing about her is stored by us and no identity document touches the chain.

Wording rules from the legal memo: two booleans and an expiry bound to an address are still personal data in the EDPB's reading, so say "no identity documents on chain, and nothing we could use to find her", never "nothing about you on chain".

## Why this and not Work Order, stated fairly

Work Order is pitchable, legible in six beats, and sits in a shape with 59 projects and a lift of 0.43. It has no asset the other 600 teams lack and no obvious continuation for this builder. The EUDI direction has one asset nobody else at the event has, a why-soon that is law, and a builder already inside the community. Its risks are equally plain: the sandbox phone run is unproven for this verifier, the jury has rewarded identity only when it was a mechanic inside a wanted product, and Tessaliq archived itself in August 2026 because accepting wallet credentials became a feature of existing identity vendors. The defensible slice is the crypto side: attestation format, wallet-key binding, the self-hosted-wallet flow.

## Kill criteria

- Sep 7: no Android sandbox presentation to this verifier with issuer trust enforced, names withheld, over 18 and DE resident on screen. Kill the direction; build Work Order.
- Sep 8: no refuse, admit, swap on Sepolia through the permissioned router (or the own-contract fallback). Kill; build Work Order.
- Sep 10: on-phone ZK stretch not green on the tripwire legs (compile on wallet-core 0.29, local proof). Kill the stretch only; tier 2 ships.
- Any date: the one-breath sentence "the token issuer or pool operator is the obliged entity, our verifier is its tool, and from July 2027 the law lets it accept exactly this credential" cannot be said on camera. Kill the direction.
- After the event: no paid pilot or letter of intent from a CASP, issuer or sale platform by 2027-03-31. Stop.
