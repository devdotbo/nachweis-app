## The two facts that decide the shape

1. **Nobody will plug us in this week or this year without a contract.** Coinbase has had one attester ever. Uniswap's permissioned-pool factory has zero adapters on mainnet. ERC-3643 has zero cross-issuer reuse in production. Every exchange stores its own self-hosted wallet proof and accepts no other VASP's. Tokenized-stock secondaries are ungated, so "buy stocks with your ID wallet" is not a barrier anyone has. The gates that exist are on permissioned instruments (EU money market funds, tokenized treasuries, RWA collateral) and at launchpads, and each is thrown by its issuer.
2. **Juries reward identity as a mechanic inside a wanted product, never as a layer.** Six finalists out of 302 identity projects, all with identity inside something a person wanted. No attestation hub, no government wallet, in 15 events of finalists.

So the pluggability is a design property we prove with our own consumers on testnet, and the video must be about something a person wants, where the ID wallet is the way in.

## The scene that survives the evidence

**Euro yield in self-custody.** A European wants to hold a tokenized euro money market fund share in her own wallet and use it in DeFi. Today that instrument (Spiko, one billion under management, 92 percent business clients, retail gated behind the issuer's own KYC and allowlist) admits only allowlisted wallets, and every venue that lists it inherits the allowlist. In the video: she tries to subscribe, refused. One tap in her state ID wallet: over 18, resident in the EU. The fund token's compliance oracle reads the attestation and admits her; she subscribes with EURC or USDC. She opens a Uniswap permissioned pool for the same token: admitted without a second check. The verifier revokes: subscription and pool both refuse in the same block. Tier B: the Austrian founder, who has no state wallet, enters the same fund through a passport chip proof, labelled as such. Ten seconds of tier two ZK, captioned.

Why this scene and not the token sale: it is the same gate (residency, age, sanctions), the instrument is one a non-expert understands ("four percent on euros, in my wallet"), it sits in the moderator's RWA domain, the two consumers (token transfer gate and pool checker) share nothing but the schema, and the product after the event is the issuer-side tooling for exactly these instruments, which are the ones with allowlists today. Why not tokenized stocks: no gate exists on the secondary market, and the primary gate is the issuer's.

What it does not claim: that Spiko or anyone accepts our attestation (they do not, say "an instrument like"); that the attestation replaces onboarding (it is eligibility; the issuer still keeps a compliance file); that nothing about her is on chain (two booleans and an expiry bound to her address, no document, nothing we could use to find her).

## The case for pivoting elsewhere, stated fairly

No demand evidence for EUDI in crypto exists: zero mentions across 5,446 showcase projects, zero use by twenty named EU crypto companies, one production eID hit (BISON via IDnow). No live product will consume the attestation. The pitch is why-soon (2027), not why-now. The phone run for this verifier has never completed. Juries have never put a compliance product in a final. Tessaliq archived itself because wallet acceptance became a feature of existing vendors. If the moderator cannot say "prove you are allowed without telling anyone who you are" and mean it, none of the above rescues the video.

Against pivoting: every alternative in the jury set is dead by the moderator's own hand, seven days remain, this is the only direction with an asset nobody else has and a continuation the builder wants, and the scene above is a wanted product, not a layer.

Lead's position (OPINION): build the euro-yield scene under the two gates. If Sep 7 fails, the honest fallback is not Work Order, which the moderator has killed, but the sandbox over-ask story on the official wallet as a small, true submission.
